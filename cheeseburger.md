    <h1>Classic CheeseBurger</h1>
    <p><strong>Fancy / Gourmet</strong>
A <em>perfectly</em> seasoned prime beef patty, seared to golden perfection, crowned with aged cheddar and nestled between a hand-toasted brioche bun. Layered with crisp garden lettuce, vine-ripened tomatoes, and a signature house sauce this is no ordinary burger.</p>
    <img src="https://bydash.com/blogs/recipes/classic-cheeseburger?srsltid=AfmBOoqFqyEJ8tOo9hQd4_MJP5U8MF_yyBYOX500XLvlW08yyWm2Azax" alt="Classic CheeseBurger">
    <h2>Ingredients</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>1 ground beef patty (80/20 blend)</li>
      <li>1 slice cheddar cheese</li>
      <li>1 soft brioche or sesame seed bun, toasted</li>
      <li>2 leaves crisp lettuce</li>
      <li>2 slices ripe tomato</li>
      <li>4–5 pickle slices</li>
      <li>2–3 rings white or red onion</li>
      <li>1 tablespoon ketchup</li>
      <li>1 tablespoon mustard</li>
      <li>1 tablespoon mayonnaise</li>
      <li>Salt and black pepper to taste</li>
    </ul>
    <h2>Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">Take your ground beef and shape it into a round patty. Season both sides with salt and black pepper.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">Heat a pan or grill on medium-high heat. Cook the patty for about 3–4 minutes on each side until fully cooked.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">Place a slice of cheddar cheese on top of the patty while it's still on the heat. Let it melt for about 1 minute.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">Slice your bun in half and toast it on the pan for 1–2 minutes until lightly golden.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">Spread ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise on the bottom bun.
</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">Layer lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles on top of the sauce.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">Place the cheesy patty on top of the veggies, close the bun, and serve immediately. Enjoy!</li>
    </ol>
